## Remediation in `765ae20` (added by the orchestrator)

| # | Change | Regression tests |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | Everything the operator tool prints from a record passes through `displaySafe`: control, invisible and direction characters are written out as visible codes; text of several lines is printed behind a margin so that it cannot pass for the tool's own lines. Text schemas refuse control characters other than tab and line feed, so a record holding them is not read. `canonicalText` removes them from model output and from an agent's rationale and instruction. The hook, call to action and claims are checked for disguised characters as the body is. | `tests/kic/fourth-review.test.ts` (four tests, one with the real tool as a child process) |
| F2 | Spending is also kept in the instance's own account (`src/workflow/spend.ts`), a file outside the campaign store, by default in the operator directory. Every step is charged there at its worst case before a model is called and corrected afterwards; per-campaign limits are checked against it, and the instance has ceilings on calls, tokens and the number of campaigns. | `tests/kic/fourth-review.test.ts` (reset record refused; instance ceilings; shared file account) |
| F3 | The hash a decision rests on covers every stored field of the draft or narrative except status and provenance, the planned post, and the brief. `show` displays the planned post, the claims and what they cite, what the brief rules out and its references. An approval or selection is void if any of these changes afterwards. Release takes two steps: `review` shows the brief's constraints, the plan and every approved post and prints a release digest; `release` must be given it. | `tests/kic/fourth-review.test.ts`, `tests/kic/processes.test.ts` |
| F4 | A response that reports no usage is charged at its allowance. | `tests/kic/fourth-review.test.ts` |

| F8 | The ledger and the spend account read own entries only. The evaluation line shown to the editor says that it is advice, unsigned and outside the digest. Evaluations remain unsigned: accepted, see the threat model. | `tests/kic/fourth-review.test.ts` |
