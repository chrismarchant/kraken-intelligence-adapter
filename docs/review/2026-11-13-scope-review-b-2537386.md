## Findings

| # | Severity | Where | Finding | Proven |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | medium | `src/mcp/server.ts`, `src/workflow/service.ts` | `kic_evaluate_drafts` accepts repeated post numbers and stores one evaluation for each, with no cap; evaluation makes no model call, so no budget applies. 2,000 repetitions grew a campaign record from 12 KB to 25.6 MB | yes |
| F2 | medium | `src/store/store.ts` | Taking over a dead owner's lock can displace a new, live lock: two waiters read the dead lock, one takes over, the other moves the first one's live lock aside and its attempt to put it back is not atomic. Two writers then commit and one update is lost while both callers are told it succeeded | yes, under forced scheduling. Six trials with real processes and kills lost nothing |
| F3 | medium | `src/store/lock.ts`, `src/workflow/spend.ts` | The same displacement in the spend account's lock loses a reservation, including the campaign's registration. Needs a crashed holder of the spend lock first | yes, under forced scheduling |
| F4 | medium | `store.ts`, `lock.ts` | A lock whose owner has died but has not been reaped by its parent (a zombie) is treated as alive for as long as the zombie exists. Under a parent that does not reap, such as a container's first process without an init, the lock blocks for ever; on the spend account that stops every model call | yes |
| F5 | medium | `src/mcp/http.ts` | The HTTP transport bounds nothing but body size: no limit on connections or requests in flight and no explicit header or request timeout. 400 unauthenticated half-sent requests made an authenticated one fail (with a lowered file-descriptor limit) | yes |
| F6 | low–medium | `src/gateway/mistral.ts` | The streamed-response reader rescans its whole buffer for every chunk and caps neither the buffer nor the text. A 64 MiB line with no newline took 26.6 s and 484 MB | yes |
| F7 | low | `mistral.ts` | Usage values from the provider are not checked beyond `completion_tokens >= 0`: a string, a negative or a fraction makes the settle step fail the record's contract, the paid-for answer is discarded and the reservation is charged in full after it expires. Errs towards charging | yes |
| F8 | low/info | `mistral.ts` | An abort during retry backoff is not seen until the sleep ends, and one more request is then sent and charged | yes |
| F9 | info | several | A malformed id is reported as an internal error, not as not found. Three messages reveal absolute server paths. A store writer can replace a record with a symlink and have the server read the target (content does not reach the caller). Temporary and moved-aside files left by a crash are never cleaned up. A spend-lock timeout during settle leaves the worst case charged | yes |
| F10 | test weakness | `tests/kic/concurrency.test.ts` | The crashed-writer test depends on natural timing and cannot catch F2. No test bounds the evaluation argument, or covers zombie owners, connection exhaustion, oversized frames or malformed usage | — |
